# Sample Printer Manual

This is an original demonstration document. It contains no vendor manual content.

## Load paper

Open the paper tray. Insert plain A4 paper below the fill line. Close the tray.

## Replace the demo cartridge

Turn off the demo printer. Open the front cover. Remove the old cartridge and insert the replacement. Close the cover before turning the printer on.

## Specifications

The demo printer accepts A4 paper and has a maximum tray capacity of 100 sheets.

## Safety

Keep the printer dry. Disconnect power before cleaning. Do not open the cover while printing.
